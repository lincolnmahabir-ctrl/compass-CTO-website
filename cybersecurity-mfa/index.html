Cybersecurity and MFA

Cybersecurity basics for districts: MFA, phishing, and safety technology

A few well-run controls prevent most incidents. These are the practices boards and superintendents can ask about directly.

Recommended practices

  1. Require multi-factor authentication. Start with staff email and key systems such as the student information, finance, and HR systems, then extend to everyone else.
  2. Run regular phishing simulations. Short quarterly simulations, with the click rate tracked over time, build awareness and provide evidence for cyber insurance renewals.
  3. Write down and practice an incident plan. Know who to call, who decides, and how staff and families are told. A short tabletop exercise each year tests the plan.
  4. Test your backups. Keep at least one copy separate from the main network, and restore from it at least once a year.
  5. Review safety technology before launch. For safety systems, confirm how alerts reach the right people after hours, what network capacity the system needs at events, and who reviews data retention terms.
  6. Communicate with families. A short technology newsletter on what the district is doing to protect accounts and data builds confidence.
