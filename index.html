What we don't do

We work alongside your team, and we're clear about where our role ends.

  • Replace your MSP or helpdesk
  • Provide legal or audit services, or act as your procurement authority
  • Guarantee grants, E-Rate funding, or certifications
  • Operate a 24/7 security operations center
  • Work directly with students, parents, or non-administrative staff; we work with district and school administration
