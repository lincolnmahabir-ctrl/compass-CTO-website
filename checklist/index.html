K-12 Technology Quick Check: 10 items district leaders can verify in an afternoon

Each item is a yes-or-no question you or your team can answer from documents you already have. A "no" or "not sure" isn't a failure. It's a good place to start a conversation. Free to use and share. No sign-up.

  1. Acceptable use policy is current

    Find the board's acceptable use policy (often Policy 815) and note when it was last revised. Does it reflect the devices and tools students and staff use today?

  2. AI guidance exists

    Is there a board policy, administrative guideline, or written guidance for staff and students on generative AI tools?

  3. Student data privacy agreements are on file

    Do you have a list of approved apps and vendors, with a signed data privacy agreement for each one that handles student data? Is that list easy for families to find?

  4. E-Rate Category 2 plan is in place

    Does someone own the plan for your Category 2 (internal connections) budget in the current five-year cycle, and do you know what has been requested so far?

  5. Email domain has DMARC protection

    Ask your IT lead whether your email domain has a DMARC record, and whether it is set beyond "monitor only" (p=none). This helps stop others from sending mail that pretends to be you.

  6. Website accessibility has been reviewed

    Has the district website been checked recently for common accessibility issues, such as missing image descriptions, low color contrast, or documents that screen readers can't read? Is there a way for people to report a problem?

  7. Technology plan is current

    Is there a written district technology plan, and do the years it covers include this school year?

  8. Cybersecurity incident plan is written down

    If an account is compromised or systems go down, is there a written plan that says who to call, who decides, and how families and staff are told? Has anyone practiced it?

  9. Staff accounts use multi-factor authentication

    Do staff email and key systems (student information, finance, HR) require a second sign-in step, such as an app prompt or code?

  10. Backups are tested

    Are critical systems backed up, is at least one copy kept separate from the main network, and has someone restored from a backup in the last year to confirm it works?
